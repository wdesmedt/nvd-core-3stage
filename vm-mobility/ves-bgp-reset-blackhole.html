vES IP aliasing · BGP reset blackhole
SR Linux EVPN-VXLAN · 3-stage NVD lab

Resetting the VM's BGP session blackholes its routes on leafs the VM isn't behind

An IP-aliasing virtual Ethernet Segment is configured on every leaf so a BGP-speaking VM can move anywhere. After the VM's PE-CE BGP session resets, leafs that carry the vES but are neither attached nor BGP peers never reinstall the VM's routes.

TriggerRT5s carrying the vES ESI withdrawn and re-advertised (VM BGP reset, or a deploy)
ImpactTraffic entering leaf3/leaf4 to 6.6.6.1 and 2001:db8::6:1 is dropped, with no recovery
ReleasesReproduced on 25.10.4, 25.10.5 and 26.7.2. Not in the 25.10.6, 26.7.1 or 26.7.3 release notes
WorkaroundAdmin-disable and re-enable the vES on the affected leafs


      

    

  


  
  

    

      
Measured impact of one BGP reset (25.10.5, after the IPv6 update-source fix)

      
Each leaf pings 6.6.6.1 and 2001:db8::6:1 from its own ipvrf-1 at 10 packets/s, sourced from its unique irb0.104 address. t = 0 is clear bgp * on vm1. Before the fix, IPv6 took 29.9 s on the healthy leafs.

    

    

    

  


  
  

    

      
vES vs RT5 GW-IP: same VM, same moves, same resets

      
vm1 carries both tenants on two VLANs and moves as one. Tenant 1 (VLAN 104, ipvrf-1, 6.6.6.1) uses an IP-aliasing virtual ES on every leaf. Tenant 2 (VLAN 204, ipvrf-2, 6.6.6.2) has no vES: the anchors export the RT5 with GW-IP 10.2.4.16, and every leaf resolves that GW-IP itself. 25.10.5, IPv6 update-source fix applied to both.

Summary
What breaks, what doesn't, and how to reproduce it.

    

      
What breaks

        
A leaf with the vES configured that is not attached (the VM isn't behind it) and has no PE-CE BGP to the VM drops the VM's RT5s after they are withdrawn and re-advertised. The RT5 is Best/Valid in the BGP RIB and the AD per-EVI routes (P=1) are present, but the route never enters ipvrf-1.

      
What keeps working

        
  • Anchor leafs leaf1/leaf2 (they learn the routes over PE-CE BGP)
    • 
        
  • Attached leafs leaf5/leaf6 (the VM is behind them)
    • 
        
  • VM moves with BGP kept up: 30 moves host3 ↔ host6, 0.8–1.0 s gap on every leaf, IPv4 and IPv6, sessions never flapped

      
Releases

        
  • Reproduced: 25.10.4, 25.10.5, 26.7.2
    • 
        
  • Not listed as a known or resolved issue in the 25.10.6, 26.7.1 or 26.7.3 release notes
    • 
        
  • 25.10.6 and 26.7.3 images aren't on ghcr yet, so they're untested

      
Workaround


leaf3# enter candidate
set / system network-instance protocols evpn ethernet-segments bgp-instance 1 ethernet-segment vES-host6-tenant1 admin-state disable
commit now
leaf3# enter candidate
set / system network-instance protocols evpn ethernet-segments bgp-instance 1 ethernet-segment vES-host6-tenant1 admin-state enable
commit now

        
Same for vES-host6-tenant1-v6, on every affected leaf. It has to be repeated after every reset.

      
Reproduce


$ docker exec clab-3-stage-evpn-vxlan-nvd-25-vm1 vtysh -c "clear bgp *"
$ vm-mobility/check.sh

Alternative that works: RT5 GW-IP

        
Tenant 2 on the same VM, with GW-IP instead of a vES and the VM subnet on every leaf, recovered on all six leafs in 3.3 s after every BGP reset. 20 moves were clean (0.8–1.0 s). See the comparison slide.

      
Also observed

        
  • IPv6 PE-CE sessions used to come back ~28 s after IPv4. vm1's SLAAC address was used as the source, so the leafs rejected vm1's connects until they connected themselves. Fixed with neighbor leafs-v6 update-source 3fff:10:1:4::16: IPv6 now recovers in 3.3 s like IPv4
    • 
        
  • Don't use host-route populate evpn as a workaround: it exports the VM /32 as an RT5 and pulls the VM's own IP to the wrong leaf
